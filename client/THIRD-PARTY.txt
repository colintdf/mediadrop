MediaDrop Ripper bundles unmodified FFmpeg 8.0.1, LAME and libdiscid.
FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org/ LGPL 2.1 or later (this audio-only build).
LAME: https://lame.sourceforge.io/ LGPL 2 or later.
mpg123 (if linked by LAME): https://www.mpg123.de/ LGPL 2.1 or later.
libdiscid: https://musicbrainz.org/doc/libdiscid LGPL 2.1 or later.
Exact source archives for these libraries and their runtime dependencies,
containing their license texts, are in Resources/Sources.
The FFmpeg build recipe and disc ID helper source are included there too.
FFmpeg's own libraries are compiled into its standalone executable; LAME and
libdiscid are dynamic libraries under Contents/Frameworks and may be replaced.
Modified executables/libraries require re-signing the bundle (an ad-hoc signature
is sufficient). No Apple account or DRM prevents replacing these components.
MusicBrainz metadata and Cover Art Archive images are fetched only on request.
No metadata or artwork service credentials are required. Artwork is previewed;
this first client does not embed it in MP3s. Use the server's Plex/Lidarr artwork
integration for its library tiles.
